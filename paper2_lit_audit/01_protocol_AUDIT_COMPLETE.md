## 0. Headline answers to the two decisive questions

**(a) Has anyone swept a temporal guard band / gap at matched training size for person identification?** — **No prior instance found in any modality.** The guard band exists as an *unswept formal construct* in dependent-data cross-validation (h-block CV, Burman–Chow–Nolan 1994; hv-block CV, Racine 2000; blocked CV, Bergmeir & Benítez 2012; purged k-fold with embargo, de Prado 2018 [UNVERIFIED DOI]) and as *spatial* dead-zone/buffer methods in geoscience (Pohjankukka 2017; Roberts 2017; Ploton 2020). The only vision paper with a graded split hierarchy is Woo (arXiv 2605.06359, May 2026) — frame-level → temporal → scene-level for intrinsic image decomposition, no identity, no sweep of gap width, no matched n_train. Li et al. (TPAMI 2021) do cross-block EEG splits but no gap sweep. Elapsed-time studies (Sarkar 2005; Matovski 2012; Best-Rowden & Jain 2018; Richings 2025) vary the gap at day–year scale between *sessions*, never within a recording at matched training size. Searches that returned nothing relevant: arXiv API ("guard band" OR "temporal gap" OR "temporal buffer" OR embargo) AND leakage AND (video OR frames): 0 hits; WebSearch ×3; Semantic Scholar ×2.

**(b) Has anyone measured a trivial-cue floor (bounding-box position/size only) for person identification?** — **No.** Nearest analogues, none of which is a metadata-only identity floor under a frame-random split: background-only re-ID (Tian et al. CVPR 2018: 37.1 % top-1 on CUHK03 with the person filled in) [V]; scene-only action recognition (Kataoka/He et al. 2016: 65.48 % on UCF101 without the human) [V]; spatio-temporal (camera + timestamp) prior lifting Market-1501 rank-1 from 91.2 % to 98.1 % (Wang et al. AAAI 2019) [V]; height/anthropometric shortcut in skeleton gait (Cătrună et al. 2024) [V]; block-correlated relabelling of EEG reaching far-above-chance accuracy (Li et al. 2021) [V]; random-label slice-level split reaching 96 % vs 50 % subject-level (Yagis et al. 2021) [V]. TVRID 2026 *speculates* that "height-related cues could dominate depth perception" but does not measure a floor [V].

**(c) Depth papers reporting BOTH frame-random and session-disjoint numbers on the same data?** — **Only MultiGait** (within-identity 70/10/20 single-session split vs session-disjoint; depth 100.0 % → 65–77 %). Every BIWI depth paper (Munaro 2014, Wu 2017, Haque 2016, Karianakis 2018, Hafner 2022, Delécluse 2026) uses the cross-day protocol by construction and never reports the same-session number; TVRID 2026 uses only a same-passage protocol. The C1 gap stated in audit 06 therefore stands, with the qualification that MultiGait now supplies the two *endpoints* for depth on one corpus.

## B. What C1 can still claim / must not claim

**Can claim (each checked against the list above):**
1. The first temporal guard-band sweep at matched training size for person identification in any modality found; the accuracy-versus-gap curve between the frame-random and session rungs has no precedent (the guard band exists only as an unswept construct in h-block/hv-block/purged CV and as spatial dead zones).
2. The first metadata-only identity floor for person identification — 13 bounding-box scalars reaching 89 % under a frame-random split — with no prior in video re-ID, gait, action, face-from-video, wildlife re-ID, EEG or HAR (nearest analogues: background-only, scene-only, spatio-temporal priors, height shortcut, random-label and block-relabelling nulls).
3. The first depth study to report every rung (R0→R4) on the *same* data, on three public/in-house corpora and three sensor technologies (Kinect v1 structured light, Azure Kinect ToF, RealSense D455 stereo), with a cross-recording (R3) rung that MultiGait does not have; MultiGait supplies only two endpoints on one own-sensor corpus.
4. Quantified inflation for vision-based *human* identification: prior quantifications are animal photos (SeaTurtleID: 18–54 pp), touch (3.8 pp EER), ECG (~15×), PPG, EEG, MRI slices (29–55 pp) and HAR — the 06 gap stands.
5. That the BIWI/TVRID depth literature has never seen its own inflated number because its protocols were cross-day or same-passage by construction; the manuscript's retracted 0.99 shows what the same data yields under the other split.
6. A ladder that operationalises Kapoor & Narayanan's temporal-leakage/non-independence categories and extends Saeb's record-wise/subject-wise and Georgiev's contiguous/random *two-way* contrasts into a *graded* instrument with a cue floor.

**Must not claim:**
1. That same-session/frame-random inflation is a discovery — Hammerla & Plötz 2015, Saeb 2017, Li 2021, Georgiev 2022, SeaTurtleID 2024, MultiGait 2026 and ECG-biometrics-bench 2026 all state it, and MultiGait already shows it for depth (100 % → 65–77 %).
2. That the guard band / block split is a new idea — h-block CV (1994), hv-block (2000), blocked CV (2012), spatial dead zones (2017) precede it; only its swept, matched-n application to person identification is new.
3. That the R3→R4 collapse measures "the effect of time" — Matovski 2012 shows time alone is nearly neutral when clothing and environment are controlled; attribute the collapse to session confounds (clothing, room, framing), consistent with C2/C4.
4. That this is the first graded split hierarchy in computer vision (Woo, May 2026, frame→temporal→scene; SeaTurtleID's closed/open time-aware splits).

## C. Positioning sentences (vs the closest works)

1. **vs MultiGait.** "MultiGait establishes, over 199 people and eight sensors, that within-session identification from depth (100 %) does not survive a session split (65–77 %); we ask the complementary question — what carries the within-session number and how fast does it decay — by inserting a temporal guard band at matched training size between the two rungs, adding a cross-recording rung, and measuring a 13-scalar bounding-box floor that alone reaches 89 %."
2. **vs SeaTurtleID2022 / WildlifeReID-10k.** "Wildlife re-identification has shown that random splits overestimate by 18–54 percentage points and that the inflation depends on the cue; we show the same for human depth identification and resolve the inflation into a within-recording temporal component and a session component."
3. **vs Li et al. (TPAMI 2021).** "Li et al. showed that block-design EEG classifiers decode time rather than stimulus by re-splitting across blocks and by relabelling; our guard-band sweep and metadata-only floor play the same diagnostic role for frame-split person identification."
4. **vs Saeb / Georgiev / Kapoor & Narayanan.** "The ladder operationalises the temporal-leakage and non-independence categories of Kapoor and Narayanan for video biometrics, turning the record-wise/subject-wise contrast of Saeb et al. and the contiguous/random contrast of Georgiev et al. into a graded curve with a cue floor."
5. **vs the BIWI depth lineage.** "Every published BIWI depth result since Munaro et al. is cross-day by construction (Training gallery, Still/Walking probe), so the field never saw the inflated same-session number; our earlier 0.99 on the same data is that number."
6. **vs Woo 2026.** "A frame→temporal→scene split hierarchy has just been advocated for intrinsic image decomposition; ours is the identity-specific counterpart, with an explicit guard-band sweep, matched training size and a metadata floor."
