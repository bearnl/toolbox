## 7. npj Digital Medicine
- Scope covers sensors/monitors and digital-medicine ethics/security; **excludes "purely observational studies, case studies, and small-scale preliminary studies"** — desk-reject risk for a 28-person lab cohort. Fully OA, APC $4,290; 10 days to first decision, 144 to acceptance.
- Verified — **the closest thematic precedent to our split-artefact finding**: Nebbia et al. 2025, "Re-identification of patients from imaging features extracted by foundation models," npj Digit Med 8, 10.1038/s41746-025-01801-0, and the Matters Arising by Engelmann, Wagner, Zhou, Keane 2026, "Near-identical images, not foundation models, explain purported re-identification of patients from medical imaging," npj Digit Med 9, 10.1038/s41746-026-02440-9. Also Varoquaux & Cheplygina 2022, npj Digit Med 5, 10.1038/s41746-022-00592-y.

1. IMWUT best of these seven (sensing + societal impact; publishes RGB-D/mmWave gait re-ID; ~8-week review; cheap OA). 2. IEEE Access = safest fallback (only venue explicitly welcoming negative results; 4-week decisions) at a prestige cost. 3. npj Digital Medicine has the closest precedent (Nebbia/Engelmann) but the small-cohort exclusion is a risk; PR/CVIU want method contributions; NMI impractical; JBHI fits the health frame but no precedent.
